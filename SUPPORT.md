# Support

**Questions go to Discussions** on the repository the question is about:

- the documentation — `nexi-devportal` → *Discussions*
- the design system, a component, a token, a story — `nexi-design-system` → *Discussions*
- a pack, a skill, the toolkit — `nexi-skills` → *Discussions*
- the dataset or the recipe — `nexi-decision` → *Discussions*

An **issue** is for something that is wrong and can be fixed: a mistake on a page, a bug, a pack that does not install.
The templates ask for what a maintainer needs to act.

A **security problem** is neither — see [SECURITY](./SECURITY.md).

There is no paid support and no service-level promise on these repositories; maintainers answer as they can.
